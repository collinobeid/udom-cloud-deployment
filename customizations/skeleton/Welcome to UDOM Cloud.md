# Welcome to UDOM Cloud! 🎓☁️

Welcome to the official University of Dodoma Cloud Storage System! 
This is your private, secure space to store coursework, share research, and collaborate with your peers.

### Getting Started:
1. **Desktop & Mobile Sync**: You can download the Nextcloud app on your phone or PC to automatically sync your files!
2. **AI Assistant**: Click the chat icon at the top right to access our private UDOM AI Assistant for help with your studies.
3. **Storage Limits**: Remember that your storage is monitored. Please keep it strictly to academic use.
